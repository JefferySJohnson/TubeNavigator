## TfL Unified API

Base: `https://api.tfl.gov.uk`. No key required; optional `app_key` query param raises the limit from ~50 to 500 req/min. Key is stored in `@AppStorage("TubeNavigator.tflAppKey")` on-device only.

Endpoints used:
- `GET /Journey/JourneyResults/{fromId}/to/{toId}?accessibilityPreference={noRequirements|stepFreeToPlatform}`
  - `fromId`/`toId` are NaPTAN IDs from StationData, e.g. `940GZZLUPAC` (Paddington). The endpoint is NOT station-only: the same `{from}`/`{to}` slots also accept a postcode, a `lat,lon` pair, or a free-text place name, and the journey comes back with walking legs to/from the nearest station (verified live 19 Nov 2026: postcode `E1 6AN` → London Bridge, 15 min with a 2-min walk to a bus stop; coords `51.5154,-0.0722` → London Bridge, 14 min with a 4-min walk to Aldgate East; `Borough Market` → Paddington, 27 min with a 9-min walk to London Bridge; Paddington → postcode `SE1 9AL`, 27 min ending in a 9-min walk). Free-text names resolve but can be fuzzy (TfL read "Borough Market" as "Southwark, Borough Market Toilets") — postcodes and coordinates are unambiguous. The app does not use this yet (Plan tab is station pickers only); it is the basis for a future location input and for pub-to-pub legs using the catalogue's pub coordinates.
  - Future time: when `useFutureTime` is on, appends `&date=yyyyMMdd&time=HHmm&timeIs=departing|arriving`. Verified live against TfL 16 Nov 2026: `date=20261217&time=1000&timeIs=departing`, Paddington → London Bridge, returns real Tube journeys (Bakerloo to Baker Street, Jubilee to London Bridge, 18 min, arrives 10:18; second option Elizabeth line, 19 min).
  - Response: `journeys[]` with `duration`, `startDateTime`, `arrivalDateTime`, `legs[]`. Each leg has `instruction.summary/detailed`, `departurePoint/arrivalPoint.commonName`, `mode.id`, and `routeOptions[0].lineIdentifier.id/name` for the line.
- `GET /Line/Mode/tube,dlr,elizabeth-line/Status`
  - Response: array of lines with `lineStatuses[0].statusSeverityDescription` and optional `reason`.
- `GET /Line/{ids}/Status/{startDate}/to/{endDate}` (dated, for Planned tab)
  - `{ids}` must be explicit line IDs (`bakerloo,central,circle,district,hammersmith-city,jubilee,metropolitan,northern,piccadilly,victoria,waterloo-city,dlr,elizabeth`) — the `/Line/Mode/...` form of this path 404s.
  - Response: lines with `lineStatuses[]`, each with `statusSeverityDescription`, `reason`, and `validityPeriods[]` (`fromDate`/`toDate` ISO 8601 with `Z`, `isNow`).
  - Client filters out `Good Service` and empty reasons, and groups identical notices across lines (a network-wide strike appears once, as "All Tube lines").

Decoding: plain `JSONDecoder`, unknown `$type` fields ignored. Times are `yyyy-MM-dd'T'HH:mm:ss` (no timezone suffix) — parsed with a `DateFormatter` in `TubeService.shortTime`. Validity periods use `ISO8601DateFormatter`.

## WeatherKit

`WeatherService.swift` — `WeatherViewModel` (`@MainActor`, `CLLocationManagerDelegate`).
- Location: `requestWhenInUseAuthorization` + `requestLocation`; delegate methods are `nonisolated` (Swift 6 mode requires it — the conformance otherwise warns about crossing into MainActor-isolated code). Falls back to Paddington (51.5166, -0.1765) when permission is denied or location fails.
- Place name: within 2 km of Paddington the strip reads "Paddington"; otherwise it reverse-geocodes with `CLGeocoder` and shows the locality (e.g. "Maineville" at home, "London" on the trip); "Your location" only as the fallback when geocoding fails. (v1.2 fix — on his iPad at home the strip read "Your location" with no place name, showing 35° Maineville weather, so he could not confirm where it was for.)
- Fetch: `WeatherService.shared.weather(for:)` — current conditions, today's high/low from `dailyForecast[0]`, hourly forecast filtered to the rest of today. All temperatures shown in Fahrenheit (`.converted(to: .fahrenheit)`).
- Attribution: "Data from Apple Weather" is shown under the strip (required by Apple).
- Setup: WeatherKit needs an **explicit App ID** for `com.rolltide.TubeNavigator` with the WeatherKit service enabled (a wildcard App ID cannot carry it), plus the `com.apple.developer.weatherkit` entitlement (in `TubeNavigator.entitlements`, wired via `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS` in the project) and the WeatherKit capability in Xcode. The App ID was registered in the portal on 16 Nov 2026 before the v2 build.
- Note: the old "WeatherKit doesn't work in the simulator" rule did not hold here — the v2 simulator run on 16 Nov 2026 returned live Paddington weather (48° drizzle, high 54°/low 41°, wind 11 mph) through WeatherKit, and v1.2 in the simulator on 16 Nov 2026 showed 47° drizzle, Paddington, high 55°/low 40°, wind 7 mph. Still verify on the real iPhone before the trip, since the simulator's location is not the phone's. The reverse-geocode path (Maineville) is not testable in the simulator — it will be confirmed on his real device.

## Maps handoff and route map

- Journey legs decode `departurePoint`/`arrivalPoint` `lat`/`lon` (added to `StopPointRef` in v2; optional, so older payloads still decode).
- `JourneyMapView` (MapKit, `Map` + `MapPolyline` per leg tinted with the line colour + start/end `Marker`s) sits at the top of `JourneyDetailView`. TfL's route geometry is straight station-to-station segments, not the true curved track — the map shows the corridor, not the exact tunnel path. MapKit tiles need signal; underground the map is blank, which is why the PDFs are bundled.
- "Open in Apple Maps" builds `https://maps.apple.com/?saddr=…&daddr=…&dirflg=r`; "Open in Google Maps" builds `https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=…&destination=…&travelmode=transit` (a universal link — opens the Google Maps app if installed, else the browser). Endpoints are `lat,lon` from the first leg's departure and last leg's arrival, falling back to station names. Verified live in the simulator on 16 Nov 2026: the handoff opened Apple Maps with the Paddington → London Bridge route.
- Journey detail footer: "Mind the gap." plus "Times are live from TfL and can change with disruptions. Check the Status tab before you travel." The detail also has a "Save this route" button (bookmark) that saves the current pair with its future time, if any.

## PDFs

Both PDFs are in the app bundle via the Resources build phase (`Bundle.main.url(forResource:withExtension: "pdf")`), rendered with PDFKit (`PDFView` via `UIViewRepresentable`, `autoScales = true`). No network needed after install. Verified present in the built app bundle with exact byte counts (v2 and v1.2). ## Pub crawl data

`PubData.swift` — **327 pubs** (v1.3): 326 imported 17 Nov 2026 from his "Historic Pubs of London Catalogue" spreadsheet (ChatGPT-built app/PWA export) plus the Spaniards Inn, which is on his original list but not in the catalogue. Audit of the spreadsheet (17 Nov 2026): 326 data rows, no blank rows, no missing names / Pub IDs / addresses / coordinates / Closest Tube values, no duplicate Pub IDs, no duplicate name+address pairs, no duplicate coordinates. 24 names repeat (four Red Lions, four Ships, three Kings Arms) but every one is a different pub at a different address — all kept. The "Area" column is blank for the 30 Jeff's-list pubs; their area is the street taken from the address. Nearest station is computed, not hand-matched: straight-line (haversine) distance from the pub's coordinates to the 97 stations, using station coordinates from TfL `StopPoint/Mode/tube,dlr,elizabeth-line` (checked 17 Nov 2026; all 97 covered). Median distance 0.37 km; 261 of 326 within 1 km; 27 over 2 km and 8 over 5 km (three around High Barnet, four around Hatch End/Harrow, one in Whetstone). 89 pubs' computed nearest differs from the catalogue's Closest Tube suggestion, mostly because the suggestion is an Overground or DLR station outside the app's list, or a nearby alternative — the catalogue suggestion is kept on each pub (`closestTube`) and shown in the detail view, so the local station is not lost. `Pub` fields: `sourceId` (the catalogue Pub ID — unique, so it is the `id`; name alone is not unique), `name`, `area`, `address`, `stationId`, `nearestKm`, `closestTube`, `whyGo`, `hours`. `distanceText` renders metres under 1 km, one decimal km above; `isFarFromTube` is `nearestKm > 2`. `PubData.firstDay` is unchanged (Borough Market, George Inn, Mayflower, Prospect of Whitby, Grapes). `PubCrawlView` has a search field (name / area / station / address) and the all-pubs list; tapping a pub opens `PubDetailView` (nearest station + distance, catalogue suggestion when it differs, address, why-go, hours, far-from-Tube note, "Plan journey to …" button). Two Ships share name + area ("The Ship", "Leadenhall Market", Hart Street vs Talbot Court) — distinct `sourceId`s, so no collision.

## State

- `TubeService` is a `@StateObject` in `ContentView`, passed as `@EnvironmentObject`.
- Favourites: `UserDefaults` key `TubeNavigator.favourites`, `[String]` of NaPTAN IDs. Defaults on first launch are the 8 trip favourites in `StationData.defaultFavouriteIds`.
- Saved routes: `SavedRoute` (id UUID, name, fromId, toId, stepFree, optional date, arriveBy; subtitle "From → To · Leave/Arrive by EEE d MMM HH:mm"), `@Published savedRoutes` persisted as JSON in `UserDefaults` key `TubeNavigator.savedRoutes`. `saveCurrentRoute` (default name "From → To"), `deleteRoute(at:)`, `renameRoute`, `loadRoute` (restores stations + step-free + future time; a route without a date plans for now). Tapping a saved route loads it and calls `planJourney()`. A saved route stores the station pair only — times are recalculated live. Persisted across relaunch (verified in the v1.2 simulator run: two test routes survived a relaunch).
- Future time: `useFutureTime`, `futureDate` (default tomorrow 10:00), `arriveBy`; `planSummary` is "now" or "Leave/Arrive by …" and drives the dynamic "Journeys — …" header.
- Accent: `@AppStorage("TubeNavigator.accentHex")`, default `"DC241F"` (Tube red); `accentColor` computed via the `Color(hex:)` extension; `accentOptions` are the 13 Tube line colours plus Crimson (Roll Tide) `9E1B32`. `TabView` gets `.tint(service.accentColor)`.
- Countdown: `TubeService.londonCountdownText()` — "N days to London" before Dec 15 2026, "Day N of the London trip" Dec 15–24, "London trip done — home for Christmas" after.
- v1.9 nearest station: `Station` gained `lat`/`lon` (TfL StopPoint/Mode/tube,dlr,elizabeth-line, fetched 19 Nov 2026 — all 97 stations present), and `StationData.nearestStation(lat:lon:)` returns the closest by haversine (the same match the pub import used offline in v1.3). `AddPlaceView`'s save passes `nearestStationId` from it, so a newly added place records in Recent and can be saved as a route like the built-ins. `TubeService.init` backfills any saved place with a nil `nearestStationId` from its coordinates (this filled in Dishoom → High Street Kensington on the v1.9 upgrade launch).
- v1.9 stale journeys: `fromEndpoint`/`toEndpoint` in TubeService have `didSet { journeys = [] }`, so changing either endpoint (a suggestion pick, a saved/recent route load, Set as From/To from a sheet, typing that nils an endpoint, swap) clears the shown journeys. `planJourney()` repopulates them. (didSet does not fire during init, so the Paddington/London Bridge defaults are safe.)
- v1.8 confirmation: `Endpoint` gained `kind: EndpointKind` (station / pub / place / found / resolved / location) and the factories `.resolved(apiId:name:)` and `.foundPlace(name:address:lat:lon:)`. PlanView's `endpointField` shows a `confirmationCaption` under each field — "✓ <detail>" when an endpoint is set (station lines+zone, pub area+station, place address, "TfL match"), orange "“<name>” not looked up — TfL will guess the place." for a raw `.location` endpoint, orange "Nothing picked yet — tap a suggestion below." when text is present but no endpoint is set. The Suggestions section has a "Look up “<query>”" row that runs `MKLocalSearch` (MapKit place search, biased to a central-London region) and offers the found place with its address; state is a `PlaceLookup` (field/query/isLookingUp/found/error) keyed by field+query so editing the text drops a stale result. **Lesson (19 Nov 2026):** the first cut used `CLGeocoder().geocodeAddressString` — an address lookup, not a place search — and "me hotel" came back empty. A place name needs `MKLocalSearch`, not a geocoder.
- Built-in places merge (v1.8): `TubeService.init` (at the END of init, after every stored property is set — a merge block placed mid-init fails to compile, "'self' used … before all stored properties are initialized") appends any built-in place whose name is not in the UserDefaults string-array key `TubeNavigator.seededBuiltInPlaces`, then records the name and calls `savePlacesToDefaults()` explicitly (didSet does not fire during init). A deliberately removed built-in is not re-added (its name stays in the seeded set); on the v1.8 upgrade launch the set is empty, so a place removed under v1.7 is re-added once.

## Build / install

1. Open `TubeNavigator.xcodeproj` on the MacBook Pro in Xcode.
2. Signing & Capabilities: confirm the team is selected (Automatic).
3. Product > Build. A successful build does not prove the install works — report build and install separately.
4. Run on iPhone (unlocked, plugged in) or simulator. Simulator first after any Info.plist change.
5. If install fails with 3002, read the NSUnderlyingError line in the dialog; check cable, unlocked phone, and remove any half-installed copy before blaming signing.
6. **Simulator control notes (learned 16 Nov 2026, updated 17–19 Nov 2026):** the simulator is presented through the Device Hub window ("iPhone 18 Pro Max – iOS 27.2", bundle `com.apple.dt.Devices`). To target it: `computer.control` `list_windows` to find its window_id, then `activate` with `app: "Device Hub"` + `window_id`. Coordinates are **normalized 0–1000 within the target window** (not pixels) — use `muse.visual_grounding` results directly, **but read the screenshot before clicking a grounded point**: on 19 Nov 2026 a grounding for the "Use 'z'" row returned the From field's position instead (the row was below the fold), and clicking it blind would have hit the wrong control. Element-based clicks (`click_element`, `click_foreground`) fail with `stale_observation` on the OCR-derived in-app elements; coordinate `click` works on tabs, toggles, pickers, alerts and fully-visible buttons, but **background clicks do not reach a button that sits under the tab bar** (e.g. "Plan journey" when the When section is expanded) — `double_click` sometimes delivers where a single click does not. **Drag works on the Plan Form and the Places List** (17 Nov 2026: a `drag` from [500,750] to [500,250] scrolled both, and a batch of 8 drags reached the bottom of the 97-station list) — this contradicts the 16 Nov finding that drag/scroll did nothing; the mirror's behaviour varies, so retry a drag before assuming content is unreachable. `scroll` (wheel) and Tab-key focus navigation do not move the lists. The journey-detail List did NOT scroll on 17 Nov (drag + wheel both no-change) — its footer ("Mind the gap.", Save button) stays below the fold in the simulator. **Typing into the simulator is unreliable with multi-character `type`:** on 17 Nov a `type` of "Barnet" landed as "A" and a follow-up "arnet" as "a" (field read "Aa"); batched single-character `key` actions (delete ×2, then b/a/r/n/e/t) typed correctly — use one `key` per character for simulator text fields. A literal `" "` is not a valid key token — send the token **`"space"`** (19 Nov 2026). **An uppercase letter needs the shift modifier** — key `"m"` with modifiers `"shift"` gives "M"; the bare key `"M"` lands lowercase "m" (the capital first letter seen earlier was the field's own autocapitalization, not the shift). Cmd+A in a simulator text field types a literal "a" instead of selecting all — use the field's × clear button (or backspaces) instead. The `fill` action (fields array of `{target, value}`) fails on in-app text fields with `stale_observation` ("The observed control could not be revalidated") — do not burn calls on it; coordinate-click the field and type with single-char keys. On 20 Nov 2026 `type` (with and without `element_number`) failed in the Device Hub with `keyboard_focus_unavailable` ("The focus is not a text field") even while the AX tree showed the field focused — the runtime's focus tracking does not follow the simulator's keyboard focus; single-char `key` actions still land. (The same failure mode appears when an approval card times out mid-typing: the keystroke is not sent at all — report it and ask Jeffery whether to retry; do not retry on your own.) Observations go stale fast after window moves/redraws — always use the freshest observation_id, and `screen.snap` observation_ids are too old for clicks (use a fresh `computer.observe` first). **If a control call is dispatched but the service restarts mid-call, or a call returns `outcome_unknown`, the Mac's computer control pauses — do not send further input; read-only inspection still works. It stays paused until the user sends a new message from that Mac. Report it to him and stop.** The computer session must be ended when the build work finishes.
