# TubeNavigator

Personal iOS app for navigating the London Underground — built for the Dec 15–24, 2026 London trip. Currently **v1.12 (build 13)** — verified on the iPhone 18 Pro simulator 23 Nov 2026 (see TECHNICAL_MANUAL.md).

- **Plan**: From/To journey planner with live TfL times, changes and step-free option — just type in From or To and pick from the suggestions that appear as you type: your saved places and favourites first, then stations, pubs, or a postcode / address / place name. A line under each field says what was actually found — a tick with the address or the station's lines, or an orange warning when the text was never looked up — so typed text alone is never confirmed. A "Look up '…'" row has the phone find a place by name, with its address. Picking a pub plans from the pub itself, with the walk to its station included. If a typed name matches more than one place, TfL answers "did you mean…?" and the app shows the shortlist to pick from instead of erroring. Journey detail has a route map and "Open in Apple Maps / Google Maps" buttons. Plan for a later time (date + time, Leave at / Arrive by) and save named routes (e.g. "Hotel → Borough Market") that re-plan live when tapped. A Recent section keeps your last few planned journeys (tap to re-plan live). Changing From or To clears the old journeys, so the list never shows the previous pair's results as if they were the new one's. A pub-crawl section plans the first-day route (Borough Market → George Inn → Mayflower → Prospect of Whitby → The Grapes) plus 327 pubs from his Historic Pubs catalogue, each matched to its nearest station, searchable, with details (why go, hours, distance) on tap.
- **Status**: live Tube / DLR / Elizabeth line status, a trip countdown ("N days to London"), plus a WeatherKit weather strip (current + rest of today, hourly) that now shows the place name (reverse-geocoded locality, e.g. Maineville at home, Paddington near the hotel).
- **Boards** (new in v1.10): live departure boards for seven stations — Paddington, Canary Wharf, King's Cross St. Pancras, Liverpool Street, London Bridge, Victoria, Waterloo. Pick a station, tap a line chip, and see the next 5 trains per platform: destination (+ "via" on the Elizabeth line), minutes away ("arriving" at a minute or less), London arrival clock time, and where the train is now where the feed gives it. Refreshes every 30 seconds, plus pull-to-refresh. Same feed logic as the TV boards, including the ghost-train filter and the composite-key dedupe that keeps DLR trains from collapsing to one row. No arrival chime in the first cut. Paddington's Elizabeth line board shows only the underground platforms A and B (v1.12) — the feed also returns the mainline station's platforms 11 and 12, late-night trains terminating at Paddington mainline, and those rows are dropped. A List / Map switch at the top of the tab (new in v1.11): Map shows the whole Elizabeth line on Apple Maps with live train dots — red eastbound, blue westbound — tap one for its destination, next stop and time. Positions are estimated from TfL arrival times; MapKit draws the map only, the dots still come from TfL.
- **Planned**: planned disruptions — strikes and engineering works, for the next 7 days or the Dec 15–24 trip. All dates and times are London time (fixed in v1.4 — they used to render in the phone's timezone, which put the Sat 19 Dec strike under a Friday header).
- **Places** (was Stations): your saved places, favourite pubs and favourite stations at the top — including the Wilde Aparthotels London Paddington hotel and ME London (336-337 Strand, for his friend's stay), each saved with its own coordinates so they never need a name lookup — plus "Add a place" for your own spots (Dishoom High Street Kensington was added this way; a place you add records its nearest station, so it shows up in Recent and can be saved as a route), the full list of 327 pubs (star any of them), and the searchable bundled station list (97 stations, works offline). An App colour accent picker (Tube line colours, crimson included), the Tube map and step-free Tube guide PDFs bundled for offline viewing, an Emergency numbers section (999 tap-to-call, text 61016 to British Transport Police, +44 800 40 50 40 to call them from a US phone), and a version row in Settings at the top of the tab ("TubeNavigator v1.12 (13)") — the same version is also a footer at the bottom, tap it for the Jeff station sign.

Data: Transport for London Unified API (`https://api.tfl.gov.uk`), free, no key required for personal use. Optional free `app_key` can be pasted in the app if rate limits ever bite.

Built with SwiftUI, iOS 17, Xcode on the MacBook Pro. Bundle `com.rolltide.TubeNavigator`. App icon is the TfL roundel with "JEFF" as the station name; the launch screen shows the same roundel.
